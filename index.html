No. 01 — The house

Not a café. Not quite a kopitiam. Somewhere warmly in between.

mug was here

Drip Oh is a neighbourhood kedai makan on Jalan Dato Mahmud, built for long lunches and slower dinners. The kitchen cooks the food Ipoh grew up on — clear-broth mee sup, creamy kari, fragrant nasi goreng with petai — next to the café plates people cross town for.

The plates come big (most can feed two), the coffee is pulled with care, and the playlist is, by popular vote, really good. Come hungry, stay for a second latte, and say hi to the owners on your way out.

Five plates we’d order again.
